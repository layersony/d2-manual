Oil Filter Joint Assembly Note

1.  Install the oil filter joint using the following procedure:

Note
•  Because a short oil filter joint is attached to a new short engine or partial engine, for vehicles with the oil cooler, replace it with a long oil filter joint.
Full length of oil filter joint
With oil cooler: 82.5—83.5 mm {3.25—3.28 in}
Without oil cooler: 36.5—37.5 mm {1.44—1.47 in}
Oil filter joint projection
With oil cooler: 61.5—66.0 mm {2.43—2.59 in}
Without oil cooler: 15.5—20.0 mm {0.611—0.787 in}
(1)  Temporarily tighten the oil filter joint.
(2)  Install two appropriate nuts (M20 × 1.5) to the oil filter joint.
bezeze00000031
(3)  Using a wrench, apply force in the directions of the arrows shown in the figure and lock the nuts.
bezeze00000032
(4)  Using a wrench, apply force in the tightening direction to the outside nut and install the oil filter joint. Verify the projection of the installed oil filter joint.
bezeze00000033
Tightening torque
30—40 N·m {3.1—4.0 kgf·m, 23—29 ft·lbf}
Oil filter joint projection
With oil cooler: 61.5—66.0 mm {2.43—2.59 in}
Without oil cooler: 15.5—20.0 mm {0.611—0.787 in}
(5)  Remove the 2 nuts installed in Step 2 of the procedure.


Oil Cooler Installation Note

Caution
•  The gasket could be damaged if the oil cooler rotates together with the tightening nut. Hold the oil cooler securely by hand when tightening the nut.

1.  Install the oil cooler so that the mark on it is oriented towards the vehicle front.

bezeze00000034

2.  Hold the oil cooler by hand.

3.  Tighten the oil cooler mounting nut.

Tightening torque
30—40 N·m {3.1—4.0 kgf·m, 23—29 ft·lbf}

Rear Oil Seal Assembly Note

1.  Apply clean engine oil to a new rear oil seal.

2.  Insert the rear oil seal to the cylinder block by hand.

3.  Install the rear oil seal using the SST.
