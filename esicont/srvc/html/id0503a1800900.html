No.4 excessive creep [FN4A-EL]

4

Excessive creep

DESCRIPTION

• Vehicle accelerates in D, S*1, L*1, M*2 ranges, and R position without depressing accelerator pedal.

POSSIBLE CAUSE

1. Engine idle speed high (transaxle system is not cause of problem)
2. Go to No.9 "Fast idle/runs on" (See NO.9 FAST IDLE/RUNS ON [ZY].)

*1 :
4AT
*2 :
Sport AT
