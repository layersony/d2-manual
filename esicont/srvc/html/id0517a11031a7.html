Selector indicator light function (sport AT) [FN4A-EL]

• The selector indicator light has a selector lever position light, and a gear position indicator light that indicates gear position when in the D and M range.
• When downshifting is cancelled in the M range, the gear position indicator light flashes two times to alert the driver that downshifting is cancelled.
