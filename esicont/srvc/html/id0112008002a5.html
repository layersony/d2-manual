Cooling system service warnings [ZY]

Warning
• Never remove the radiator cap or the coolant filler cap or loosen the radiator drain plug while the engine is running, or when the engine and the radiator are hot. Scalding coolant and steam may shoot out and cause serious injury. It may also damage the engine and cooling system.
• Turn off the engine and wait until it is cool. Even then, be very careful when removing the cap. Wrap a thick cloth around it and slowly turn it counterclockwise to the first stop. Step back while the pressure escapes.
• When you are sure all the pressure is gone, press down the cap still using the cloth, turn it, and remove it.
• Hot engines and engine coolant can cause severe burns. Turn off the engine and wait until it and the coolant are cool before draining the engine coolant.
