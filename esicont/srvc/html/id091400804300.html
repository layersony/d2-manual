Liftgate latch and lock actuator removal/installation

1. Detach the liftgate outer handle rod.

2. For vehicles without the keyless entry system, detach the liftgate key cylinder rod.

3. Remove the bolts, then remove the liftgate latch and lock actuator.

4. Install in the reverse order of removal.
