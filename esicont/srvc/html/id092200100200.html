Instrument cluster outline

•  A CAN system has been adopted for the control signals of the input/output communication circuit of the meters, gauges, and warning and indicator lights. (See CONTROLLER AREA NETWORK (CAN) SYSTEM OUTLINE.)
• LEDs have been adopted for all warning and indicator lights installed on the instrument cluster.
• A trumpet-type horn with a spiral and resonant pipes has been adopted.
