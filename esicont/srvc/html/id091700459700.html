Front door trim removal/installation

1. Disconnect the negative battery cable.

2. Remove the inner garnish.

3. Open the cap using a small flathead screwdriver, then remove screw A.

4. Remove the inner handle cover.

5. Remove the recess cover on vehicles with a recess cover.

6. Remove the screw B.

7. Disengage clips A from the body using a fastener remover.

8. Pull the front door trim upward, then disengage clips B from the front door trim.

9. Disconnect the power door mirror switch connector and the power window main switch connector at driver's side. Disconnect the power window subswitch connector at passenger's side.

10. Remove clips B from the body using a fastener remover, then install them to the front door trim.

11. Install in the reverse order of removal.
