Valve mechanism outline [ZY]

• There are two intake ports and two exhaust ports for each cylinder. There are a total of 16 valves directly driven by two camshafts.
• A variable valve timing mechanism has been adopted that insures the best valve timing depending on the driving conditions by constantly changing the phase of the intake port side camshaft.
