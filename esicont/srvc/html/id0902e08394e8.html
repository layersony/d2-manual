DTC inspection [Instrument cluster]

WDS

1. Connect the WDS or equivalent to the DLC-2.

2. Verify if any DTCs are displayed.

• If any DTCs are displayed, perform the troubleshooting according to the corresponding DTC inspection.

3. Disconnect the WDS or equivalent.
