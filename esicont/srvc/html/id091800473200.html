Auto leveling control module removal/installation

1. Disconnect the negative battery cable.

2. Remove the glove compartment No.1. (See GLOVE COMPARTMENT NO.1 REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

3. Disconnect the a connector.

4. Remove the nut, then remove the auto leveling control module.

5. Install in the reverse order of removal.
