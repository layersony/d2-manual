Headlight leveling system outline

• Allows adjustment of the headlight optical axis (changes due to varying cargo and passenger conditions) from the vehicle interior, in order to prevent blinding of oncoming vehicles.
• The headlight optical axis can be freely adjusted by setting the headlight leveling switch between 0-3 ("0" is the maximum upward angle, "3" is the maximum downward angle).
