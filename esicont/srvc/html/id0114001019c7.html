Fuel tank construction [ZY]

• Capacity is 45 L {12 US gal, 9.9 lmp gal}.
• One rollover valve is built in, and the check valve (two-way) is pressed in the evaporative hose on the fuel tank. For the rollover valve and the check valve (two-way), refer to the emission system.
• Made of hard plastic for weight reduction.
