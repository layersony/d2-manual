Outer mirror glass removal

1. Press area A of the mirror glass so that area B moves outward.

2. Disengage clip A while lifting up the inside of the mirror glass holder.

3. Pull the mirror glass holder outward, then disengage clips B by pulling them out.

4. Remove the mirror glass holder and the mirror glass as a single unit.
