Security light: DTC 12 (WDS: DTC B2103)

DTC 12 (B2103)

Coil failure.

POSSIBLE CAUSE

• Defective Coil
• Poor connection of coil connector

Diagnostic procedure

Step

Inspection

Action

-
INSPECT CONNECTOR CONNECTION
• Is connector of coil and PCM connected securely?
Yes
Replace coil.
(See COIL REMOVAL/INSTALLATION)
No
Connect connector securely.
