DTC P0660 [ZY]

DTC P0660

Variable intake air control circuit malfunction

DETECTION CONDITION

• The PCM monitors the variable intake air control signal. If the PCM turns the variable intake air shutter valve actuator to open or close but the voltages do not coincide with the PCM signal voltages, the PCM determines that the variable intake air control circuit has a malfunction.
• The PCM could not perform the self-test for the variable intake air control circuit.
Diagnostic support note
• This is a continuous monitor (Other).
• The MIL does not illuminate.
• PENDING CODE is available if the PCM detects the above malfunction condition.
• FREEZE FRAME DATA is not available.
• The DTC is stored in the PCM memory.

POSSIBLE CAUSE

• Variable intake air shutter valve actuator malfunction
• Connector or terminal malfunction
• Open circuit in wiring harness between variable intake air shutter valve actuator terminal A and PCM terminal 2AJ
• Short to power supply in wiring harness between variable intake air shutter valve actuator terminal A and PCM terminal 2AJ
• Short to ground in wiring harness between variable intake air shutter valve actuator terminal A and PCM terminal 2AJ
• Open circuit in wiring harness between variable intake air shutter valve actuator terminal B and PCM terminal 2AI
• Short to power supply in wiring harness between variable intake air shutter valve actuator terminal B and PCM terminal 2AI
• Short to ground in wiring harness between variable intake air shutter valve actuator terminal B and PCM terminal 2AI
• PCM malfunction

• Turn the ignition switch to the ON position (Engine off).

- Variable intake air shutter valve actuator terminal B (wiring harness-side) and body ground
• Is the voltage B+?
Yes

No
Go to the next step.

- Variable intake air shutter valve actuator terminal B (wiring harness-side) and body ground

Yes
Repair or replace the wiring harness for a possible short to ground, then go to Step 9.
No
Go to the next step.
