Parking light bulb removal/installation

1. Disconnect the negative battery cable.

2. To remove the front combination light (LH) bulbs, remove the fresh-air duct. (Vehicles with Dual Headlights) (See INTAKE-AIR SYSTEM REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [ZY].)

Note
• When removing the fresh-air duct, be careful not to damage the sponge attached to the fresh-air duct.

3. Remove in the order indicated in the table.

1
Connector
2
Socket
3
Parking light bulb

4. Install in the reverse order of removal.
