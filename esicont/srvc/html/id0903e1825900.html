No.8 abnormal sound of the cassette tape (play backwards)

8

Abnormal sound of the cassette tape (Reverse playback)/Possible DTC: -

Troubleshooting hints
• Cassette tape is malfunctioning
• Cassette tape player is malfunctioning
Note
• Reverse playback is when the cassette tape is played in the opposite direction of the current playback direction.

Diagnostic procedure

STEP

INSPECTION

ACTION

1
• Replace with a cassette tape known to be good.
• Is the sound normal?
Yes
The cassette tape player is normal. Explain to the customer that the cassette tape is malfunctioning.
No
Replace the cassette tape player.
