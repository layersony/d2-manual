Oil pump disassembly/assembly [ZY]

Warning
• Hot engines and engine oil can cause severe burns. Turn off the engine and wait until it and the engine oil have cooled.

1. Remove the oil pump. (See OIL PUMP REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [ZY].)

2. Disassemble in the order indicated in the table.

1
Oil pump cover
2
Inner rotor, outer rotor
(See Inner Rotor, Outer Rotor Installation Note.)
3
Plunger plug
4
Plunger spring
5
Control plunger
6
Oil pump body

3. Assemble in the reverse order of disassembly.

Inner Rotor, Outer Rotor Installation Note

1. Align the punch marks of the inner and outer rotor and assemble.
