Rear washer nozzle removal/installation

1. Disconnect the negative battery cable.

2. Remove the high-mount brake light. (See HIGH-MOUNT BRAKE LIGHT REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

3. Squeeze the tabs, then remove the rear washer nozzle.

4. Install in the reverse order of removal.

5. Adjust the rear washer nozzle. (See REAR WASHER NOZZLE ADJUSTMENT.)
