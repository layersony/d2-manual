Disc pad (front) replacement

1. Remove in the order indicated in the table.

2. Install in the reverse order of removal.

3. Depress the brake pedal a few times, then verify that the brakes do not drag.

1
Brake hose clip
2
Retaining clip
(See Retaining Clip Installation Note)
3
Cap
4
Bolt
5
Boot
6
Caliper component
7
Disc pad
