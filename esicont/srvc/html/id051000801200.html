Clutch pedal removal/installation

1.  Disconnect the negative battery cable.

2.  Separate the steering shaft.

3.  Remove the accelerator pedal. (See ACCELERATOR PEDAL REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [ZY].)

4.  Remove in the order indicated in the table.

5.  Install in the reverse order of removal.

am2zzw00005295

1
Brake switch connector
(See BRAKE PEDAL REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
2
Clutch switch connector
3
Brake switch
(See BRAKE PEDAL REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
4
Clutch switch
(See Clutch Switch Installation Note.)
5
Joint pin
(See BRAKE PEDAL REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
6
Pedal assembly
(See Pedal Assembly Installation Note.)
7
Pedal pad


Pedal Assembly Installation Note

1.  Begin to tighten with nut A.

am2zzw00005296

2.  After installing the pedal assembly, inspect the clutch pedal. (See CLUTCH PEDAL INSPECTION. )


Clutch Switch Installation Note

1.  Insert the new clutch switch into hole of the pedal bracket until the switch stops.

2.  Rotate the clutch switch 45° clockwise.

am2zzw00005297

3.  Verify that the clutch switch is locked securely.
