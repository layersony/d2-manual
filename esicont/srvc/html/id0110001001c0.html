Mechanical outline [ZY]

Features

Improved engine performance
• A variable valve timing mechanism has been adopted.
Reduced engine weight
• Main parts (cylinder head and cylinder block) are made of aluminum alloy.
Reduced engine noise and vibration
• The cylinder head is made of aluminum alloy.
• A silent timing chain has been adopted.
• A highly rigid aluminum cylinder block and lower cylinder block have been adopted.
• A crankshaft pulley with torsional damper has been adopted.
• A pendulum type engine mount has been adopted.
Improved serviceability
• A serpentine type drive belt has been adopted.
• Drive belt tension is adjusted automatically with an auto-tensioner.
• A timing chain has been adopted to eliminate the need for its replacement.
• An engine front cover with a service hole has been adopted. (for unlocking the chain adjust ratchet and securing the tensioner arm.)
