Headlight bulb removal/installation

Vehicle with Dual Headlights

1. Disconnect the negative battery cable.

2. To remove the front combination light (LH) bulbs, remove the fresh-air duct. (See INTAKE-AIR SYSTEM REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [ZY].)

Note
• When removing the fresh-air duct, be careful not to damage the sponge attached to the fresh-air duct.

3. Remove in the order indicated in the table.

1
Connector
2
Cover
3
Headlight bulb

Caution
• A halogen bulb generates extremely high heat when it is illuminated. If the surface of the bulb is soiled, excessive heat will build up and the life of the bulb will be shortened. When replacing the bulb, hold the metal flange, not the glass.

4. Install in the reverse order of removal.
