Cooling fan motor removal/installation [ZY]

1. Disconnect the negative battery cable.

2. Remove the cooling fan. (See RADIATOR REMOVAL/INSTALLATION [ZY].)

3. Remove in the order indicated in the table.

1
Cooling fan
2
Cooling fan motor
3
Radiator cowling

4. Install in the reverse order of removal.
