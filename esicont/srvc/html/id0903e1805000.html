No.1 no sound from the cassette tape

1

No sound from the cassette tape/Possible DTC: 00:Er01, 00:Er03, 00:Er10

Troubleshooting hints
• Tape head gathers oxide residue from cassette tape
• Cassette tape is malfunctioning
• Cassette tape player is malfunctioning

Diagnostic procedure

STEP

INSPECTION

ACTION

1
• Replace with the cassette tape known to be good.
• Does the cassette tape player sound?
Yes
The cassette tape player is normal. Explain to the customer that the cassette tape is malfunctioning.
No
Go to the next step.
2
• Clean the tape head using a good quality head-cleaning tape.
• Does the cassette tape produce sound?
Yes
Explain the repairs (head cleaning) to the customer.
No
Replace the cassette tape player.
