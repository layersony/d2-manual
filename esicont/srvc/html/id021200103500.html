Wheels and tires outline

• In consideration of the environment, a balance weight made of steel has been adopted to reduce amount of lead used in the vehicle.
• An adhesive-type balance weight is fastened on the inner side of the wheel. Since it is not visible from the styled side of the wheel, the design of the wheel is favoured (15-inch aluminum alloy wheel).
