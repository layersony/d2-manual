DTC 07: Er08

MD

DETECTION CONDITION

• Blank unrecorded MD is inserted.

POSSIBLE CAUSE

• Blank unrecorded MD

Diagnostic procedure

Action

• Inform customer that blank unrecorded MD was inserted into MD player.
• Insert recorded MD into MD player.
