Dashboard disassembly/assembly

1. Remove the passenger-side air bag module. (SeePASSENGER-SIDE AIR BAG MODULE REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

2. Remove the audio unit.

3. Remove the climate control unit.

4. Disassemble in the order indicated in the table.

5. Assemble in the reverse order of disassembly.

1
Dashboard harness
2
Dashboard member
3
Upper dashboard
4
Center ventilator grille
5
Side ventilator grille
6
Lower dashboard
