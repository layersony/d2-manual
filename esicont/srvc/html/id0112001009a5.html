Cooling fan construction [ZY]

• Plastic cooling fans have been adopted for weight reduction.
• Electric cooling fans, operated according to a fan control signal from the PCM, have been adopted. Due to this, engine noise is reduced and rapid warming-up is possible.
• The cooling fan and cooling fan motor are attached to the radiator cowling.
