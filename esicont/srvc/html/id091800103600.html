Discharge headlight outline

• A discharge headlight has been adopted on vehicles with quadruple headlights (low-beam) for wider area illumination compared to previous headlights. In addition, by emitting white light resembling the sunlight, night visibility while driving has been improved.
• The gas discharge bulb is efficient with low power consumption and high luminosity.
