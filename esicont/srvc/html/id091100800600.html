Rear door unit removal/installation

1. Remove the rear door glass.

2. Remove the inner handle.

3. Remove the rear door speaker.

4. Disconnect the power window motor connector and rear door lock actuator connector.

5. Remove the rear door harness from the rear door unit.

6. Remove screws A and the fastener.

7. Remove screw B, then remove the rear door actuator tab from the rear door unit.

8. Pull the rear door unit slightly outward, and while keeping the rear power window regulator or rear manual window regulator from catching on the rear door, remove the door unit from the door.

9. Pull out the inner handle cable and door lock knob cable from the hole.

10. Remove the rear power window regulator or rear manual window regulator.

11. Install in the reverse order of removal.
