Exhaust system outline [ZY]

• The exhaust pipe has been shortened by locating the exhaust manifold on the rear of the engine to improve the exhaust efficiency. In addition, the distance to the catalyst has been shortened to prevent a decrease in exhaust gas temperature, promoting the earlier activation of the catalyst at cold-engine start for improved purification efficiency of the exhaust gas.
