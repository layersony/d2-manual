ABS HU/CM inspection

1. Remove the battery and battery tray.

2. Disconnect the HU/CM connector.

3. Install the battery.

4. Attach the tester lead to the ABS HU/CM harness side connector, then inspect voltage, continuity or resistance according to the table.
