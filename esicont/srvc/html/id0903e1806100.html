No.3 CD player/changer does not play the CD/no sound

3

CD player/changer does not play the CD/No sound

Possible DTC:03:Er07, 03:Er10, 06:Er07, 06:Er10, 10:E07, 10:Er10, 22:Er07, 22:Er10

Troubleshooting hints
• CD is inserted upside down
• Audio units is malfunctioning
• Defective CD (e.g., cracked, badly bent, rough edges, scratch, dirty CD, condensation)
• Non-conventional discs (e.g., 8 cm (3 in) CD, 8 cm (3 in) disc adapter, heart-shaped disc, octagonal disc)
