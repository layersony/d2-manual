Thermostat inspection [ZY]

Warning
• During inspection, the thermostat and water are extremely hot and they can cause severe burns. Do not touch the thermostat and water directly.

1. Inspect the following.

• A closed valve at room temperature
• The opening temperature and lift of the valve
- If there is any malfunction, replace the thermostat.
Thermostat initial-opening temperature
80-84 °C {176-183 °F}
Thermostat full-open temperature
95 °C {203 °F}
Thermostat full-open lift
8.5 mm {0.33 in} or more
