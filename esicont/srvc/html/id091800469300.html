Combination switch disassembly/assembly

1. Disassemble in the order indicated in the table.

1
Light switch
(See Light Switch (Wiper and Washer Switch) Assembly Note.)
2
Wiper and washer switch
(See Light Switch (Wiper and Washer Switch) Assembly Note.)
3
Body

2. Assemble in the reverse order of disassembly.

Light Switch (Wiper and Washer Switch) Assembly Note

1. Set the light switch (wiper and washer switch) groove and hook at the positions indicated in the figure, and insert them into the body.

2. Insert the light switch until the light switch tab is engaged with the body.
