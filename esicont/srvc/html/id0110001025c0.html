Drive belt construction [ZY]

• A serpentine-type drive belt with a V-ribbed belt to drive the supplemental units has been adopted. This shortens the engine length and improves serviceability.
• A drive belt auto tensioner with an embedded coil spring has been adopted to automatically maintain optimal drive belt tension.
