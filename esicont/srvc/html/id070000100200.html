HVAC features

Improved Air Conditioning Performance
• A sub-cooling system integrating the condenser and receiver/drier has been adopted to facilitate evaporator operation. This system also reduces the number of parts and the amount of refrigerant.
Reduce Weight
• The A/C unit integrating the cooling unit and heater unit has been adopted to reduce weight.
