Shift-lock inspection

1. Turn the ignition switch to the ON position.

2. Verify that the selector lever is in the P position.

3. Depress the brake pedal and verify that the selector lever can be shifted from P position.

• If there is any malfunction, inspect the interlock cable. (See INTERLOCK CABLE INSPECTION.)
