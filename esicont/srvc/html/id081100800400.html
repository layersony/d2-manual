Front buckle removal/installation

1. Perform the following procedure for vehicles with a buckle switch.

(1) Remove the front seat. (See FRONT SEAT BELT REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)
(2) Disconnect the buckle switch connector.
(3) Remove the clips that secure the harness from the front seat.

2. Remove the bolt, then remove the front buckle.

3. Install in the reverse order of removal.
