Front scuff plate removal/installation

1. Pull the front scuff plate upward, then disengage clips A, pin B, and tabs C, D.

2. When removing the front scuff plate on the driver's side, pull the fuel-filler lid opener lever upward, then remove the front scuff plate.

3. Install in the reverse order of removal.
