No.1 vehicle does not move in D, S, L, M ranges, or in R position [FN4A-EL]

1

Vehicle does not move in D, S*1, L*1, M*2 ranges, or in R position

DESCRIPTION

• Vehicle does not move when AP depressed.

POSSIBLE CAUSE

• If the vehicle does not move in D, S*1, L*1, M*2 ranges or R position, basically, the malfunction is in the ATX. (Vehicle will move even with a malfunction in the PCM.) Since a malfunction is in the sensor circuit or output circuit is the cause of the malfunction in the ATX, inspect the sensors, output circuit, and the related harnesses.
1. Clutch slipped, worn (D, S*1, L*1, M*2 ranges-Forward clutch, R position-Reverse clutch, Low and reverse brake)
• Line pressure low
• Shift solenoid D malfunction
• Shift solenoid E malfunction
• Shift solenoid A malfunction
• Shift solenoid B malfunction
• Pressure control solenoid malfunction
• Body GND malfunction
• Control valve body malfunction
2. Selector lever malfunction
3. Parking mechanism not properly operation
4. Torque converter malfunction
Note
• Before following troubleshooting steps, make sure that Automatic Transaxle On-Board Diagnostic and Automatic Transaxle Basic Inspection are conducted.
