Rear bumper disassembly/assembly

1. Remove in the order indicated in the table.

1
Reflector
2
Nut
3
Fastener (Sport type)
4
Clip (Sport type)
5
Bracket (Sport type)(SeeBracket Removal Note.)
6
Rear bumper fascia

2. Install in the reverse order of removal.

Bracket Removal Note

1. Remove the clip.

2. Remove the fastener.

3. While moving the bracket in the direction of the arrow (1), remove it in the direction of the arrow (2) and detach tabs A.
