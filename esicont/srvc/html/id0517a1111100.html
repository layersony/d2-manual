Powerflow outline [FN4A-EL]

• In the powertrain mechanism, hydraulic pressure is transmitted from the control valves or shift solenoid A, B, or C (duty-cycle type) to operate the clutches and brakes, and the planetary gear changes the gear ratio according to the vehicle driving condition.
