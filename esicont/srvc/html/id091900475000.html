Windshield wiper motor inspection

1. Connect battery positive voltage to terminal B or A and ground to terminal C, and inspect operation of the windshield wiper motor as indicated in the table.

• If there is any malfunction, replace the windshield wiper motor.

Terminal

Operation

B
High
A
Low

2. Disconnect battery positive voltage from the motor terminal B while the wipers are operating.

3. Verify that the wipers do not stop in the park position.

4. Short between the motor terminals D and A, and connect battery positive voltage to the motor terminal E.

5. Verify that the wipers operate at low speed again, then stop in the park position.

• If there is any malfunction, replace the windshield wiper motor.
