Security light: DTC 11 (WDS: DTC B1681)

DTC 11 (B1681)

Coil signal is not received.

POSSIBLE CAUSE

• Defective wiring harness
• Defective coil
• Defective PCM

Diagnostic procedure

Step

Inspection

Action

1
INSPECT POWER SUPPLY CIRCUIT OF COIL
• Disconnect the coil connector.
• Turn the ignition switch to the ON position.
• Measure voltage at terminal D of coil connector.
• Is voltage more than 8 V?
Yes
Go to the next step.
No
Repair wiring harness.
2
INSPECT WIRING HARNESS BETWEEN COIL AND GROUND
• Turn the ignition switch to the LOCK position.
• Inspect wiring harness between coil connector terminal C and ground for the following:
- Short to power supply
- Open circuit
• Is wiring harness okay?
Yes
Go to the next step.
No
Repair wiring harness.
3
INSPECT COIL INPUT SIGNAL CIRCUIT
• Connect coil connector.
• Turn the ignition switch to the ON position.
• Measure voltage at terminal B of coil connector.
• Is voltage more than 8 V?
Yes
Go to Step 5.
No
Go to the next step.
4
INSPECT COIL INPUT SIGNAL CIRCUIT
• Turn the ignition switch to the LOCK position.
• Disconnect PCM connector.
• Turn the ignition switch to the ON position.
• Measure voltage at terminal 1AO of PCM connector.
• Is voltage more than 8 V?
Yes
Replace PCM and reprogram immobilizer system.
(See IMMOBILIZER SYSTEM COMPONENT REPLACEMENT/KEY ADDITION AND CLEARING)
No
Go to the next step.
5
INSPECT COMMUNICATION CIRCUIT (INPUT) FOR CONTINUITY
• Turn the ignition switch to the LOCK position.
• Disconnect coil and PCM connectors.
• Is there continuity between coil connector terminal B and PCM connector terminal 1AO?
Yes
Go to the next step.
No
Repair wiring harness.
6
INSPECT COIL INPUT SIGNAL CIRCUIT
• Measure resistance between coil connector terminal B and ground.
• Is resistance more than 10 kilohms?
Yes
Replace coil.
(See COIL REMOVAL/INSTALLATION)
No
Repair wiring harness.
7
INSPECT COIL OUTPUT SIGNAL CIRCUIT
• Connect coil and PCM connectors.
• Turn the ignition switch to the ON position.
• Measure voltage at terminal A of coil connector.
• Is voltage more than 8 V?
Yes
Replace coil.
(See COIL REMOVAL/INSTALLATION)
No
Go to the next step.
8
INSPECT COIL OUTPUT SIGNAL CIRCUIT
• Turn the ignition switch to the LOCK position.
• Disconnect coil connector.
• Turn the ignition switch to the ON position.
• Measure voltage at terminal A of coil connector.
• Is voltage more than 8 V?
Yes
Replace coil.
(See COIL REMOVAL/INSTALLATION)
No
Go to the next step.
9
INSPECT COMMUNICATION CIRCUIT (OUTPUT) FOR CONTINUITY
• Turn the ignition switch to the LOCK position.
• Disconnect PCM connector.
• Is there continuity between coil connector terminal A and PCM connector terminal 1AS?
Yes
Go to the next step.
No
Repair wiring harness.
10
INSPECT COIL OUTPUT SIGNAL CIRCUIT
• Measure resistance between PCM connector terminal 1AS and ground.
• Is resistance more than 10 kilohms?
Yes
Replace PCM and reprogram immobilizer system.
(See IMMOBILIZER SYSTEM COMPONENT REPLACEMENT/KEY ADDITION AND CLEARING)
No
Repair wiring harness.
