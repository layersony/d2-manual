Tire house trim removal/installation

1. Remove the trunk side trim. (See TRUNK SIDE TRIM REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

2. Remove the rear scuff plate. (See REAR SCUFF PLATE REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

3. Remove the fastener.

4. Remove tab from the C-pillar trim.

5. Pull the tire house trim outward, then disengage clip A from the body.

6. Install in the reverse order of removal.
