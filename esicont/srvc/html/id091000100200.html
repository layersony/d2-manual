Body panel outline

• The body shell has been designed to increase vehicle structural rigidity and reduce shock to the body, thus improving safety.
• The crushable zone, an H-shaped frame (triple-H structure), absorbs impact energy and prevents it from reaching the occupants.
• A steel bumper reinforcement has been adopted for improved safety.
• A resin shroud panel has been adopted for improved serviceability.
