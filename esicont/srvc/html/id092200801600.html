Instrument cluster disassembly/assembly

Caution
• If the instrument cluster is dropped or the print plate is damaged, the system will not work properly and it may be the cause of trouble or malfunctions.

1. Disassemble in the order indicated in the table.

1
Meter unit
2
Case
3
Lens

2. Assemble in the reverse order of disassembly.
