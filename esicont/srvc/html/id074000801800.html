Climate control unit removal

1. Disconnect the negative battery cable.

2. Disconnect the air intake, air mix and airflow mode wires from each wire clamp and link.

3. Remove the center panel. (See CENTER PANEL MODULE REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

4. Release the two tabs and pull the climate control unit outward you.

5. Disconnect the climate control unit connectors and remove the climate control unit.
