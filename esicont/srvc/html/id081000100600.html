Curtain air bag module function

• During a lateral collision to the vehicle, the air bag operates (deploys) after receiving an operation signal from the SAS control module to soften the impact to the side of the head of the driver and other passengers (passenger-side and rear outboard-seated passenger).
• Operates (deploys) in conjunction with the side air bags.
