Fuel-filler lid adjustment

1. Measure the gap and height difference between the fuel-filler lid and the body, and verify that they are within the specification.

2. Loosen the fuel-filler lid installation bolts, and reposition the fuel-filler lid.

Clearance
a:2.0-4.0 mm {0.08-0.15 in}
b:-0.5-1.5 mm {-0.019-0.059 in}

3. Tighten the bolts.
