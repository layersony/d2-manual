Child-restraint seat anchor construction

• ISOFIX anchors for securing an ISOFIX child-restraint seat, and tether strap anchors have been adopted.
Caution
• Installation procedure varies with the type of child-restraint seat. When installing a child-restraint seat, be sure to follow the prescribed procedure for each type.
