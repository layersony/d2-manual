EGR valve removal/installation [ZY]

1. Disconnect the negative battery cable.

2. Drain the engine coolant from the radiator. (See ENGINE COOLANT REPLACEMENT [ZY].)

3. Disconnect the EGR valve connector.

4. Remove in the order indicated in the table.

1
Engine coolant hose
2
EGR valve
(See EGR Valve Installation Note.)

5. Install in the reverse order of removal.

EGR Valve Installation Note

1. Replace the EGR valve gasket.
