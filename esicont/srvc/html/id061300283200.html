EPS control module inspection

1. Remove the front scuff plate (LH). (See FRONT SCUFF PLATE REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

2. Remove the front side trim (LH). (See FRONT SIDE TRIM REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

3. Attach the tester lead to the underside of the control module connector and inspect the voltage and continuity according to the Terminal Voltage Table (Reference) indicated in the table.
