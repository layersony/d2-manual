No.5 wavering sound and/or poor sound quarity of the cassette tape

5

Wavering sound and/or poor sound quality of the cassette tape/Possible DTC: -

Troubleshooting hints

• Cassette tape is malfunctioning.
• Cassette tape player is malfunctioning.

Diagnostic procedure

STEP

INSPECTION

ACTION

1
• Replace with a cassette tape known to be good.
• Is the sound normal?
Yes
Go to the next step.
No
Replace the cassette tape player.
2
• Fast-forward and rewind the original tape.
• Is the sound normal?
Yes
The cassette tape player is normal.
Note
• If the tape is fast-forwarded/rewound fast-forward/rewind for a short period repeatedly, the tape playing speed may be temporarily changed.
No
The cassette tape player is normal.
