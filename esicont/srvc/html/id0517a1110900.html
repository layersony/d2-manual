Shift solenoid D and E (on/off type) outline [FN4A-EL]

• A compact, light-weight three-way solenoid has been adopted for shift solenoids D and E to reduce consumption discharge amount.

Shift solenoid

Function

Shift solenoid D
Switches the bypass valve and 3-4 shift valve.
Shift solenoid E
Switches the low and reverse shift valve and TCC control valve.
