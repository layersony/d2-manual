Center roof antenna removal/installation

1. Disconnect the negative battery cable.

2. Remove the headliner. (See HEADLINER REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

3. Remove in the order indicated in the table.

1
Antenna rod
2
Nut
3
Antenna feeder
4
Center roof antenna

4. Install in the reverse order of removal.

5. After installation, verify that the rubber of the center roof antenna is installed to the roof panel with no space between them.
