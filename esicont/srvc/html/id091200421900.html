Outer mirror garnish removal

1. Remove the power outer mirror glass from the power outer mirror. (See OUTER MIRROR GLASS REMOVAL.)

2. Pinch the clip ends using pincers and detach the clips by pushing them forward.

3. Grasp the upper side of the power outer mirror garnish and remove it by pulling it in the direction of the arrow.
