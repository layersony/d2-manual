Electric power steering (EPS) outline

• Pinion assist EPS has been adopted for all models.
• The EPS provides smooth handling from low to high speeds due to the superior steering feel and vehicle speed response control.
• Since the electric power assist system does not require a power steering oil pump, engine load has been reduced, improving fuel economy.
