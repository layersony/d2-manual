No.26 starter does not work [FN4A-EL]

26

Starter does not work

DESCRIPTION

• Starter does not work even when P or N position.

POSSIBLE CAUSE

• Selector lever misadjustment
• TR switch mis-adjustment
• Open or short circuit in TR switch
