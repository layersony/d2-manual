Rear axle pre-inspection

Wheel Bearing Play Inspection

1. Inspect the wheel bearing play in the same way as the front axle. (See WHEEL HUB, STEERING KNUCKLE PRE-INSPECTION.)

• If it exceeds the specification, replace the wheel bearing.
Rear axle maximum wheel bearing play
0.05 mm {0.002 in}
