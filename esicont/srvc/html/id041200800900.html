Parking brake lever inspection

Stroke Inspection

1. Pull the parking brake lever a few times.

2. Inspect the parking brake stroke by pulling up slowly on the parking brake lever at position A, 50 mm {1.97 in} from the end of the lever, with a force of 98 N {10 kgf, 22 lbf} and count the number of notches (clicking sound).

• If not within the specification, adjust the parking brake lever.
Parking brake lever stroke when pulled at 98 N {10 kgf, 22 lbf}
3 notches or less
