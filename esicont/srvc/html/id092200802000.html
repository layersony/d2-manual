Instrument cluster repair

Note
• The speedometer or tachometer needle may continue to rotate clockwise until it passes maximum graduation. If a customer complains of this concern, repair the needle position according to the following steps:
• Also, if the speedometer, tachometer, fuel gauge, or the water temperature gauge needle will not return to zero even when the ignition switch is turned to the LOCK position, the needle position can be repaired by performing the following steps:

1. Verify the concern.

2. Turn the ignition switch to the LOCK position.

3. Disconnect the negative battery cable.

4. Connect the negative battery cable.

5. Turn the ignition switch to the ON position.

6. Verify that the needle returns to zero.

• If the needle does not return to zero, replace the instrument cluster.
