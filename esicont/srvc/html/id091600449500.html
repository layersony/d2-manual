Extractor chamber removal/installation

1. Remove the rear bumper. (See REAR BUMPER REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

2. Remove the trunk side trim.

3. Squeeze and disengage tabs A, and remove the extractor chamber from the vehicle side.

4. Remove the extractor chamber.

5. Install in the reverse order of removal.
