Windshield wiper motor removal/installation

1. Disconnect the negative battery cable.

2. Remove the windshield wiper arm and blade. (See WINDSHIELD WIPER ARM AND BLADE REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

3. Remove the cowl panel. (See COWL PANEL REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

4. Remove the front suspension tower bar. (See FRONT SUSPENSION TOWER BAR REMOVAL/INSTALLATION.)

5. Remove the vacuum hose clip from front wiper bracket.

6. Remove in the order indicated in the table.

1
Bolt
2
Windshield wiper motor
3
Connector
4
Clip

7. Install in the reverse order of removal.

8. Adjust the windshield wiper arm and blade. (See WINDSHIELD WIPER ARM AND BLADE ADJUSTMENT.)
