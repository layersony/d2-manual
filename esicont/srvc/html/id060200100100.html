DLC-2 construction

• A DLC-2 connector conforming to ISO (International Organization for Standardization) standards has been added.
• Shape and terminal arrangement as stipulated by the ISO 15031-3 (SAE J1962) international standard has been adopted for this connector. The connector has a 16-pin construction that includes the CAN_H, CAN_L, GND1, GND2 and B+ terminals.

Terminal

Function

Serial communication Lo terminal
CAN_H

GND1
