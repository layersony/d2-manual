Oil Seal (Primary Shaft) Disassembly Note

1.  Remove the oil seal using a flathead screwdriver.

a6e5112m012


Bearing Race (Secondary Shaft) Disassembly Note

1.  Remove the bearing race by lifting it and the funnel out together.

adj5112m008


Roll Pin Disassembly Note

1.  Align the groove for removal of the clutch housing pin with the position of the roll pin, then tap the pin out using a pin punch.

ada5112m009


Control Rod Disassembly Note

1.  Move the control rod in the direction of the arrow.

a6e5112m005

2.  Using a flathead screwdriver and hammer, make a hole on the cap.

a6e5112m106

3.  Pry the seal cap off, putting the flathead screwdriver into the hole, made in Step 2, from the inside of the transmission.

a6e5112m107


Oil Seal (Differential) Disassembly Note

1.  Remove the oil seal using a flathead screwdriver.

a6e5112m016


Bearing Race (Differential) Disassembly Note

1.  Remove the bearing outer race using the SST.

ada5112m010


Reverse Lever Shaft Disassembly Note

1.  Remove the roll pin using pliers.

adj5112m011

2.  Protect the reverse lever shaft with a rag and use pliers to remove the shaft.

adj5112m012


Bearing Race (Transaxle Case) Disassembly Note

1.  Remove the bearing race using the SST.

ada5112m013


Oil Seal (Shift Lever Component) Disassembly Note

1.  Remove the oil seal using a flathead screwdriver.
