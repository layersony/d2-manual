Centrifugal balance clutch outline

• The centrifugal balance clutch mechanism, which cancels the centrifugal oil pressure, is adopted for improve clutch control.
• A bonded seal piston (press-worked component of a piston and a seal) is adopted for each clutch and brake to reduce the piston size and weight.
