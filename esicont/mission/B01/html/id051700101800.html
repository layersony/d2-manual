Automatic transaxle features

Realization of excellent shift quality
• Electronic pressure-adjusting control of line pressure by a liner type solenoid (pressure control solenoid) adopted
• Electronic control (direct electric shift control) of clutch pressure by duty-cycle type solenoids (shift solenoid A, B, and C) adopted
• Centrifugal balance clutch chamber adopted
High efficiency, compactness, and light weight
• Miniature trochoid gear type oil pump with torque converter direct drive adopted
• Bonded seal piston adopted
• Tow-step final gear drive mechanism adopted
Improved reliability, reduced NVH (noise, vibration and harshness)
• Double arranged gears with a single planetary gear unit adopted
