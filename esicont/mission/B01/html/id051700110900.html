Shift solenoid D, and E (on/off type) outline

• Compact, light-weight three-way solenoid are adopted for shift solenoids D and E to reduce consumption discharge amount.

Shift solenoid

Function

Shift solenoid D
Switches bypass valve and 3-4 shift valve
Shift solenoid E
Switches low and reverse shift valve and TCC control valve
