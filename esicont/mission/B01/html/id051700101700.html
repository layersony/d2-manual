Automatic transaxle outline

• An electronically controlled automatic transaxle with FN4A-EL type four-speed torque converter clutch combining advanced electronic and mechanical technologies has been adopted.
• In the FN4A-EL type automatic transaxle, the part count is greatly reduced to lessen its size and weight. Also, a well-balanced powertrain mechanism with high reliability is adopted to improve marketability.
